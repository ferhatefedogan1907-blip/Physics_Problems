# EM Spectrum – Solution

To list electromagnetic radiation in order of increasing wavelength, we start from the shortest wavelength and move toward the longest wavelength.

## Correct Order

1. Gamma rays  
2. X-rays  
3. Ultraviolet  
4. Infrared  
5. Microwaves  
6. Radio waves  

## Final Answer

\[
\text{Gamma rays} \rightarrow \text{X-rays} \rightarrow \text{Ultraviolet} \rightarrow \text{Infrared} \rightarrow \text{Microwaves} \rightarrow \text{Radio waves}
\]

## Explanation

- Gamma rays have the **shortest wavelength** and the **highest energy**.
- Radio waves have the **longest wavelength** and the **lowest energy**.
- As wavelength increases, frequency and energy decrease.
