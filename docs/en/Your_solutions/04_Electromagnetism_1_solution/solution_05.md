# Problem 5: Field Levitation

## Problem

What electric field strength is required to make a proton levitate against Earth's gravity at the surface of the Earth?

Given:

$$
m_p = 1.67 \times 10^{-27} kg
$$

$$
q = e = 1.6 \times 10^{-19} C
$$

$$
g = 9.8 m/s^2
$$

---

## Idea

For the proton to levitate, it must not accelerate upward or downward.

This means the net force on the proton must be zero.

Gravity pulls the proton downward:

$$
F_g = mg
$$

The electric field must create an upward electric force:

$$
F_e = qE
$$

For equilibrium, these two forces must have equal magnitude:

$$
F_e = F_g
$$

So,

$$
qE = mg
$$

Now we solve for the electric field strength:

$$
E = \frac{mg}{q}
$$

---

## Calculation

Substitute the values:

$$
E = \frac{(1.67 \times 10^{-27})(9.8)}{1.6 \times 10^{-19}}
$$

First calculate the numerator:

$$
1.67 \times 9.8 = 16.366
$$

So,

$$
E = \frac{16.366 \times 10^{-27}}{1.6 \times 10^{-19}}
$$

Now divide the numbers:

$$
\frac{16.366}{1.6} = 10.22875
$$

Now divide the powers of ten:

$$
\frac{10^{-27}}{10^{-19}} = 10^{-8}
$$

Therefore,

$$
E = 10.22875 \times 10^{-8}
$$

In scientific notation:

$$
E = 1.022875 \times 10^{-7} N/C
$$

Approximately,

$$
E \approx 1.02 \times 10^{-7} N/C
$$

---

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{E \approx 1.02 \times 10^{-7} N/C}
$$

The electric field must point upward.

Since the proton has positive charge, the electric force is in the same direction as the electric field.

---

## How to explain during class

The main idea is force balance.

If the proton levitates, it stays still vertically. That means the upward electric force must balance the downward gravitational force.

So I write:

$$
qE = mg
$$

Then I solve for the electric field:

$$
E = \frac{mg}{q}
$$

After substituting the values, the required electric field is

$$
E \approx 1.02 \times 10^{-7} N/C
$$

Because the proton is positively charged, the electric field must point upward to create an upward force.
