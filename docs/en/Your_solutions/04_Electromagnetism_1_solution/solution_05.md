## Solution

For the proton to levitate, the electric force must balance the gravitational force.

The gravitational force is

$$
F_g = mg
$$

The electric force is

$$
F_e = qE
$$

For equilibrium:

$$
F_e = F_g
$$

So,

$$
qE = mg
$$

Solving for electric field strength:

$$
E = \frac{mg}{q}
$$

Now substitute the values:

$$
E = \frac{(1.67 \times 10^{-27})(9.8)}{1.6 \times 10^{-19}}
$$

Calculate the numerator:

$$
1.67 \times 9.8 = 16.366
$$

So,

$$
E = \frac{16.366 \times 10^{-27}}{1.6 \times 10^{-19}}
$$

$$
E = 10.23 \times 10^{-8}
$$

$$
E = 1.02 \times 10^{-7}\ \mathrm{N/C}
$$

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{E \approx 1.02 \times 10^{-7}\ \mathrm{N/C}}
$$

The electric field must point upward so that the electric force on the positive proton is upward and balances gravity.
