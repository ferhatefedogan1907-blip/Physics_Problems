# Problem 8: Lorentz Force

## Problem

A charged particle has charge

$$
q = 2 \times 10^{-19} C
$$

and enters a magnetic field

$$
B = 0.5 T
$$

with speed

$$
v = 10^6 m/s
$$

The velocity is perpendicular to the magnetic field.

Find the magnitude of the Lorentz force acting on the particle.

---

## Idea

The magnetic force on a moving charged particle is

$$
F = qvB \sin\theta
$$

Here, the velocity is perpendicular to the magnetic field.

So,

$$
\theta = 90^\circ
$$

and

$$
\sin 90^\circ = 1
$$

Therefore, the formula becomes

$$
F = qvB
$$

---

## Calculation

Substitute the values:

$$
F = (2 \times 10^{-19})(10^6)(0.5)
$$

First multiply the numbers:

$$
2 \times 0.5 = 1
$$

Now multiply the powers of ten:

$$
10^{-19} \times 10^6 = 10^{-13}
$$

Therefore,

$$
F = 1 \times 10^{-13} N
$$

---

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{F = 1.0 \times 10^{-13} N}
$$

The force is perpendicular to both the velocity and the magnetic field.

---

## How to explain during class

The main point is that the particle enters the magnetic field perpendicularly.

Because of this, the angle between velocity and magnetic field is

$$
90^\circ
$$

So the sine term becomes one.

Then the Lorentz force formula becomes simple:

$$
F = qvB
$$

After substituting the values, the force is

$$
F = 1.0 \times 10^{-13} N
$$

The direction is perpendicular to both velocity and magnetic field, but the question asks only for the magnitude.
