## Solution

The magnetic part of the Lorentz force is given by

$$
F = qvB\sin\theta
$$

Since the particle enters the magnetic field perpendicular to the field,

$$
\theta = 90^\circ
$$

and

$$
\sin 90^\circ = 1
$$

So the formula becomes

$$
F = qvB
$$

Now substitute the values:

$$
F = (2 \times 10^{-19})(10^6)(0.5)
$$

First multiply the numbers:

$$
2 \cdot 0.5 = 1
$$

and combine the powers of ten:

$$
10^{-19} \cdot 10^6 = 10^{-13}
$$

Therefore,

$$
F = 1 \times 10^{-13}\ \mathrm{N}
$$

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{F = 1.0 \times 10^{-13}\ \mathrm{N}}
$$

The force is perpendicular both to the velocity of the particle and to the magnetic field.
