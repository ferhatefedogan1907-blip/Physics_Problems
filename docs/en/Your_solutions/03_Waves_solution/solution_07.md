## 7. Standing Wave Modes

### Question
A standing wave with four antinodes is produced on a string of length $L = 80 \text{ cm}$. What is the wavelength of this wave?

### Solution

In a standing wave on a string, each antinode represents half of a wavelength.

So, if there are 4 antinodes, the string contains 4 half-wavelengths:

$$
L = 4 \cdot \frac{\lambda}{2}
$$

This simplifies to:

$$
L = 2\lambda
$$

So:

$$
\lambda = \frac{L}{2}
$$

Convert the length to meters:

$$
L = 80 \text{ cm} = 0.80 \text{ m}
$$

Now calculate:

$$
\lambda = \frac{0.80}{2} = 0.40 \text{ m}
$$

### Final Answer

- $\lambda = 0.40 \ \text{m}$

This means that four antinodes fit into the string length, which corresponds to two full wavelengths.
