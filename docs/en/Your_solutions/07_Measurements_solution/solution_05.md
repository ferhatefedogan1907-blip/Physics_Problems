# Percentage Uncertainty Calculation

## Given

A time measurement is recorded as:

\[
t = 5.45 \pm 0.22 \text{ s}
\]

where:

- Measured value = 5.45 s
- Absolute uncertainty = 0.22 s

## Formula

Percentage uncertainty is calculated using:

\[
\text{Percentage Uncertainty} = \frac{\text{Absolute Uncertainty}}{\text{Measured Value}} \times 100
\]

## Substitution

\[
\text{Percentage Uncertainty} = \frac{0.22}{5.45} \times 100
\]

\[
= 0.04037 \times 100
\]

\[
= 4.04\%
\]

## Answer

\[
\boxed{4.04\%}
\]

The percentage uncertainty of the measurement is **4.04%**.
