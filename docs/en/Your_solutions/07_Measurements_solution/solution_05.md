# Percentage Uncertainty Calculation

## Given

A time measurement is recorded as:

t = 5.45 ± 0.22 s

where:

- Measured value = 5.45 s
- Absolute uncertainty = 0.22 s

The question asks for the percentage uncertainty of the measurement.

---

## Formula

Percentage uncertainty is calculated using:

Percentage Uncertainty = (Absolute Uncertainty / Measured Value) × 100

This formula expresses the uncertainty as a percentage of the measured value.

---

## Substitution

Substituting the given values:

Percentage Uncertainty = (0.22 / 5.45) × 100

= 0.04037 × 100

= 4.04%

---

## Answer

Percentage Uncertainty = 4.04%

Therefore, the percentage uncertainty of the measurement is **4.04%**.
