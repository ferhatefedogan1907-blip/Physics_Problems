# Question 6 – Instrument Precision

## Given

A digital thermometer reads:

T = 25.4°C

Assume that the uncertainty is half the value of the last digit.

---

## Step 1: Determine the smallest increment

The last digit is in the tenths place, so the smallest increment shown by the thermometer is:

ΔT = 0.1°C

---

## Step 2: Calculate the uncertainty

For a digital instrument, the absolute uncertainty is half of the smallest increment:

ΔT = 0.1 / 2 = 0.05°C

---

## Step 3: Write the final result

Therefore, the temperature measurement is:

T = (25.4 ± 0.05)°C

---

## Answer

**Absolute uncertainty:**

ΔT = ±0.05°C

**Measured temperature:**

T = (25.4 ± 0.05)°C
